## Review disposition

Independent reviewer (fresh context): 0 Critical, 7 Important, 7 Minor. All headline numbers in the first report matched the result files.

**Fixed, each with a test that failed first:**
- **I3** — search by email matched every customer whose phone contained a digit of the query, which inflated L0 leaks. Fixed and rerun on all models.
- **I4** — email ids shifted when the adaptive attacks were inserted, so cached replays broke. Ids now derive from the scenario id; rerun.
- **I2** — a name together with city or spend was not counted as personal data (Flash sent 10 names with cities and LTV to an "insurer"). Oracle and policy detector updated.
- **I5** — refusals and leaks on benign mail were counted as "done". Benign mail now has its own outcomes: done, refused, leaked, blocked, pending approval, not done.
- **I6** — L2 capability blocks were recorded without customer ids, so they looked harmless. The would-be ids are now computed.
- **M2** (phones written with spaces), **M3** (cost column note) and **M4** (stale traces on rerun) are fixed.
