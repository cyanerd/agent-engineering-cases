## Описание

Case A3: a local model is worse than a paid one on some tasks, and paying for the big one on every request is not an option. Which routing features, how to tune them on metrics, and how to prove quality did not drop.

**Stand**
- 480 tasks with automatic checks:
  - classify: support tickets, half of them hard;
  - extract: JSON by schema;
  - math: GSM8K sample;
  - qa: short answers over a text.
- A seeded 50/50 train/test split.
- Models:
  - local `qwen3:8b` without reasoning (3 samples) and with reasoning;
  - `deepseek-v4.1-flash`;
  - `deepseek-v4-pro` as the expensive tier.
- Policies:
  - all cheap;
  - all expensive;
  - a per-type rule;
  - a cascade grid of 96 configurations: JSON validity, sample agreement, types always sent to expensive.
- The working-point rule and the 2 p.p. non-inferiority margin were fixed before test was looked at.

**Results on test**

| Policy | Accuracy | Share to expensive | $ per 1000 requests | p50 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chosen cascade | 98.3% | 52% | 0.20 | 3.8 s |
| All expensive | 99.2% | 100% | 0.60 | 3.5 s |

- Non-inferiority is **not proven**: [−2.1; 0] p.p. against a −2 p.p. margin.
- Shadow-mode sizing: about 130 comparisons, or about 1300 router requests at 10% shadow.
- With reasoning on, the cheap tier needs only 0.8% expensive calls but has a p50 of 23 s.

**Fix included:** the first "fast" run did not turn reasoning off, because Ollama 0.40 ignores `/no_think` in the prompt. `@aec/llm` now sends `reasoning_effort` (test included). The bad answers were discarded.

## Review disposition

An independent reviewer with fresh context found 1 Critical, 4 Important and 6 Minor issues. All numbers reproduce from results/. The reviewer found no train/test leakage: the margin and the selection rule were committed before any answers existed. The grader is sound.

**Fixed, each with a test that failed first:**
- **C1 + I3.** The shadow-mode size came from a formula that assumed a true difference of 0, with a different α than the offline test. It is replaced by a simulation on the test pairs with the same decision rule (`shadowPower`). At the observed −0.8 p.p., 130 comparisons prove non-inferiority only 33% of the time. Reaching 80% takes about 1000 comparisons, or about 500 if the shadow covers only the requests left to the cheap model.
- **I1.** Requests routed to the expensive model by their type were charged the cheap samples' latency. Now they are not. The cascade's p50 is 3.3 s, not 3.8 s, so it is faster than all-expensive.
