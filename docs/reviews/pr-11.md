## Описание

Case A1: a booking agent where 5% of runs get a different site layout. What checks, in what order, where a human confirms, what each step costs.

**Stand**
- A mock ticket site with a main layout and four realistic broken ones:
  - swapped date and time columns;
  - US numeric dates;
  - ungrouped rows numbered from the stage, with the row label on the right;
  - an unlabelled promo row of another event in the sessions table.
- An LLM agent (`deepseek-v4.1-flash`) driving a real browser by ref.
- A brittle scripted bot written against the main layout, as a no-model baseline.
- The checks ladder is replayed on the recorded carts and orders.

**Results**

| | Runs | Wrong orders |
|---|---|---|
| Flash | 600 (300 intents × free text and strict schema), $5.3 | 4, mostly a wrong ticket count; 1 of 297 on broken layouts |
| Script | 300 | 4, all a session of another event from the promo row |

- The invariants (qty, price, date) stopped all of Flash's wrong orders.
- Only the independent cart reader stopped the script's: price and count were fine, so the invariants passed them.
- Neither check stopped a correct order.
- Confirming every purchase above 15 000 ₽ adds a question in 4.6-5.9% more runs and catches nothing extra.

**Not done**
- qwen3:8b as a weak agent: the context grows to ~30k tokens against a 32k window, at ~10 min per run. The ruling is in the ledger, and the report says so.
